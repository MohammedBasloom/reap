The studies and valuations produced by REAP are preliminary, indicative analyses intended for initial screening and decision support. They are not accredited reports and should not be relied upon for official, regulatory, financing or legal purposes.

01What the platform produces

The platform produces financial models and indicative valuations from the inputs and assumptions you enter. Their purpose is to form a quick first view of an opportunity's feasibility or approximate value, so you can judge whether it merits deeper study.

The methodologies draw on internationally recognised approaches — including the three IVS-aligned valuation approaches — but that does not make the output an accredited report. An accredited report requires a licensed valuer, a physical inspection, and documentation issued under that valuer's professional responsibility.

02Not advice

Neither the platform nor its output constitutes investment, financial, legal or tax advice, and it is not a substitute for consulting an accredited valuer or a qualified adviser before making any decision.

03Results depend on your inputs

The quality of any output depends on the accuracy of the inputs and assumptions entered. REAP supplies indicative defaults oriented to the Saudi market to make starting easier, but these remain general assumptions that should be reviewed and adjusted to the specific opportunity.

Verifying those inputs and reviewing the results before acting on them remains your responsibility alone.
