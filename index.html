FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a finance or real-estate background?
No. Every field comes with plain-language hints and realistic market defaults, and the platform flags assumptions that look off. REAP is designed for non-specialists first.
Is REAP free to use?
Yes — sign up free and your analyses are saved to your account, or explore as a guest without registering (nothing is saved in guest mode).
What methods does the valuation platform use?
The three IVS-aligned approaches: sales comparison with adjustments, income capitalisation, and depreciated replacement cost — reconciled into a single value with a confidence range.
Can I export and share my results?
Yes — both platforms export a print-ready summary report as PDF, in Arabic or English.
